09 / commercial evidence

A revenue bridge, not a volume promise.

Retained opening revenue+Existing-customer expansion+In-year new-customer revenue+Hardware adjustments

If starting from opening run rate, subtract churn and contraction. Keep ARR, recognised revenue and bookings distinct. New wins = qualified enquiries × opportunity acceptance × win rate; in-year revenue also depends on activation timing and revenue per win. No real forecast has been entered.

Data that can be joined

Source/version → asset/version → campaign/UTM → permitted enquiry ID → deduplicated account → opportunity/stage → win/activation → reconciled revenue.

Join only with identifier evidence. Record unknown attribution, keep anonymous engagement separate and count primary revenue once. Sourced and influenced views are separate.

Freeze the test before release

Question, audience, one principal change, primary outcome, denominator, baseline, comparison, cutoff and observation window. Business owner accepts the versioned specification.

Proposed checks: 7 days for distribution; 28 days for comparable engagement; observed sales-cycle/activation lag for revenue. These are diagnostic windows, not success thresholds.
