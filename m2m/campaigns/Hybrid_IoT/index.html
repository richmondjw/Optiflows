Week 1 · Asset tracking

Post platform: M2M One company page (Tuesday). James personal reshare Wednesday.

Recommended image: A shipping container or road-train trailer parked on the edge of a regional yard at dusk, dust track leading away, no visible mobile tower in shot. Overlay in top-left: "Cellular when you can. Satellite when you must." Cool blue-grey tones so the palette stays on-brand.

The trailer does not stop where the tower stops.

Asset tracking works beautifully on cellular right up until the asset moves off the main freight route, into a yard on the outskirts of a regional town, or across a border overnight. Then the pings stop, the dashboard goes yellow, and someone spends a morning on the phone finding out where a $40,000 piece of plant actually is.

Cellular when you can. Satellite when you must. One supplier. One solution. One point of contact.

Week 2 · Agtech

Post platform: M2M One company page (Wednesday). James personal reshare Thursday.

Recommended image: Aerial shot of a working property — pivot irrigation circles or a stripe of paddocks running out to the horizon, with a small cluster of buildings near frame edge to show scale. Muted greens and earth tones.

You can plan the property. You cannot plan the tower.

The strong cluster of sensors near the shed and the pivot works fine. It is the long tail of paddocks, tanks, and stock movements beyond the tower that goes dark. The soil probe on the back block might be the most useful sensor on the farm, and it is also the one you can't reach.

Cellular when you can. Satellite when you must. One supplier for both.

#Agtech #PrecisionAg #IoT #Iridium #M2M #Farming

Week 3 · Remote monitoring

Post platform: M2M One company page (Wednesday). James personal reshare Thursday.

Recommended image: A lonely pump station, water tank, or rural substation at golden hour, sky filling the top two-thirds of the frame, one small indicator light glowing on the equipment.

The pump station is fine. Until it isn't.

By the time the SCADA screen shows red, the pump has been dry for four hours, or the substation alarm has been sitting quietly with nowhere to go. Cellular carries 90% of SCADA telemetry across Australia. The gap is the sites the tower doesn't quite reach.

Cellular for the reads. Satellite for the alarm. One dashboard, one supplier.

#SCADA #RemoteMonitoring #Utilities #IIoT #Iridium #M2M

Week 4 · Field safety / lone worker

Post platform: M2M One company page (Tuesday). James personal reshare Wednesday.

Recommended image: A single high-vis figure walking a ridge, coastline, or forest track — silhouette or back-of-shot, no face visible. Wide landscape behind.

Three of the four verticals in our Hybrid IoT series follow the same rule. This one is the exception.

Field safety is the one vertical where we size satellite up.

If the person carrying the device presses SOS, the packet has to get through. It cannot queue behind other traffic. It cannot fail because the plan was scaled for cost. It cannot depend on a tower being in range.

Cellular when you can. Satellite when you must. Never size down the safety leg.
