Photography doctrine

  • Named place: show the actual place.
  • Named event: use organiser-cleared or commissioned coverage.
  • Abstract frame: capture it at the real location and do not overclaim.
  • Search and scraping: research only, never a licence.
  • Generative tools: no reconstructed place, crowd, venue or person.
  • Production: preserve credit, licence and release evidence.
