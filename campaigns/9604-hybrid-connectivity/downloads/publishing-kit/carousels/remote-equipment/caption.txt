Remote equipment monitoring earns its value when it changes a maintenance or operating decision. That makes message priority more important than message volume.

What condition requires attention now? What can wait for the cellular path to recover? What should be compressed into a small exception payload? And what local behaviour must continue even if neither network is available?

Those questions turn ‘hybrid connectivity’ from a feature into an operating design.
