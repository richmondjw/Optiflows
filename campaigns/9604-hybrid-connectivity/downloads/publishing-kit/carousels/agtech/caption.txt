Distributed agriculture creates a practical connectivity question: which readings need to leave the field when terrestrial coverage is intermittent or absent?

The right answer is usually selective. Routine readings may be stored, batched or retried. A threshold event—water level, equipment state or environmental condition—may deserve a different path and priority.

Hybrid IoT is strongest when message value, power budget, antenna conditions and operating geography are designed together. It is not a substitute for that work.
