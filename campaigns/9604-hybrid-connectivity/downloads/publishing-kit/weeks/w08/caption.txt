For a lone-worker or field-safety product, connectivity is important—but it is only one part of the safety system.

A responsible design defines message priority, retry behaviour, acknowledgement, escalation, device-state visibility and what the worker should do when communications are unavailable. A satellite path may extend the operating design beyond dependable cellular coverage; it should never be presented as a guarantee of delivery or safety.

Build the communications dependency into the risk assessment from the start.
