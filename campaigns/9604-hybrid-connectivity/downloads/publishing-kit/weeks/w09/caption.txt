The Iridium Certus 9604 combines LTE-M cellular, Iridium Short Burst Data and GNSS in a 16 × 26 × 2.4 mm module. Iridium describes independent control of the cellular and satellite subsystems and a unified AT-command interface.

For an engineering team, the practical value is design flexibility and a smaller integration footprint—not a black-box promise. Antennas, power, firmware, payloads, service plans, certification and operating conditions still belong in the design review.
