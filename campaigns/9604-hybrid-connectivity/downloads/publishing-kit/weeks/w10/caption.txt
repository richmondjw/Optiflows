A useful Hybrid IoT conversation starts before the module selection.

Bring seven inputs:
1. Operating regions and movement patterns
2. Known or suspected coverage boundaries
3. Message types and payload sizes
4. Reporting frequency and urgency
5. Power, enclosure and antenna constraints
6. Retry, acknowledgement and fallback behaviour
7. Commercial and service assumptions

We have packaged the questions into a practical readiness guide for product and solution teams.

Download the guide: https://optiflows.com.au/campaigns/9604-hybrid-connectivity/downloads/hybrid-iot-readiness-guide.pdf
