A missed location update, threshold alert or safety escalation does not carry the same consequence as routine telemetry. That distinction should shape the connectivity design.

Before adding a satellite path, rank the messages your operation produces:
• Which ones are time-sensitive?
• Which ones can wait and retry?
• Which ones trigger a human decision?
• What is the operational cost when one never arrives?

The answer creates a clearer brief for reporting frequency, retry logic, power use and the role of each network layer.
