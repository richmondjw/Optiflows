    10 · Evidence and release control

    Proof before promise.

    The pack separates primary product evidence, existing campaign context and generated creative. It also keeps operational contradictions visible instead of silently treating them as approval.

    Release sequence

    1. Recheck claims, dates, destinations and source evidence.
    2. Accept named marketing, engineering and sales reviews.
    3. Release only through an authorised human or governed executor.
    4. Read back the public result, attribution and lead response.
