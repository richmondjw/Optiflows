# Spring is for decisions

## Review state

This is the complete source manuscript for a four-week Peninsula Insider campaign. James selected creative direction B, **Field Specimens / Decision Cabinet**, on 6 September 2026. That decision authorises completion of this review package, not publication.

The campaign contains twenty primary assets: four web anchors or controlled refreshes, four web dispatches, four email drafts, four five-slide feed carousels and four three-frame Story sequences. The audience dates below are proposals. Nothing is scheduled, sent or live because of this document.

Release, email send, social publishing, paid distribution, subscriber or CRM mutation and commercial commitments remain separately governed. Every date-sensitive fact must be rechecked within 24 hours of any authorised audience action. Unknown data is `N/A`, never zero.

## Campaign proposition

**Promise:** one useful Peninsula decision each week.

**Reader:** locals and Melbourne visitors who want a good day without turning leisure into logistics.

**Editorial position:** Peninsula Insider is most useful when it narrows the field, states the trade-off and puts the primary source within reach. It should not behave like an exhaustive directory or pretend Max has visited somewhere he has not.

**Visual proposition:** each decision is arranged as a tactile field specimen. The illustration supplies mood and an ownable monthly grammar. Exact facts, labels, dates, distances, calls to action, identity and accessibility text stay in the deterministic information layer.

## S3-WEB — complete anchor refresh

**Search title:** Mornington Peninsula school holidays: choose the day shape | Peninsula Insider

**Meta description:** Plan one school-holiday day around its energy: active-first, rain-safe or low-friction, with live programme and booking checks before leaving.

**Headline:** School holidays without the scramble

**Standfirst:** Choose the shape of the day before the venue. One anchor, one flexible second move and a clean way out beats a list nobody can finish.

### First, read the energy

Victoria’s published government-school holiday period runs from Monday 21 September to Friday 2 October 2026. Term 4 begins Monday 5 October.[V1] You do not need fourteen perfect days. You need a few days whose demands match the people in them.

Before opening tabs, choose one of three shapes: active-first, rain-safe or low-friction. That decision removes most of the wrong options.

### Active-first

Use this shape when movement is the point. Put one outdoor or physical activity first, while energy is available. Make everything after it optional.

Do not attach a café, beach, playground and scenic drive by default. Check weather, access, age suitability, booking and any current safety advice with the operator. If the first activity works, lunch can be the ending.

### Rain-safe

Use this shape when the forecast is uncertain or the group needs a contained plan. The Shire’s current school-holiday programme includes library, art and workshop listings across the holiday period.[S1] Treat that page as a live inventory, not a guarantee of places.

Choose one age-matched session, confirm whether booking is required, and keep the travel radius small. If it sells out or changes, return to the programme rather than relying on an old screenshot.

### Low-friction

Use this shape when the cost of organising is already too high. Pick the nearest suitable activity with a clear start, a clear end and minimal equipment. One booked hour can be enough.

Low-friction is not a lesser day. It is the right design when a full itinerary would make everyone manage the plan instead of enjoying it.

### The public-holiday check

Friday 25 September is the Victorian public holiday before the AFL Grand Final.[V2] Public-holiday trading and operating arrangements can differ. Do not assume a usual Friday timetable applies; check each operator directly.

### The five-minute lock

Before leaving, confirm:

1. the activity is running on the exact date;
2. age and supervision requirements fit;
3. booking or capacity is understood;
4. access, cost and what to bring are current; and
5. the fallback requires less effort than the original plan.

Then stop researching. One anchor is a plan.

# Week 4 — The Spring reset weekend

## Commission

**Reader decision:** Which two fixed points make 3–4 October feel planned without overbooking it?

**Content decision:** consolidate existing market, picnic and sunset material into a date-aware weekend service. Do not create another generic spring list.

**Distinctive angle:** fix Saturday morning and protect Sunday evening; deliberately leave the middle loose.

**Confirmed boundary:** Mornington Peninsula Shire lists Hill & Ridge Community Market at Red Hill Recreation Reserve on Saturday 3 October 2026, 9 am–2 pm.[H1] Business Victoria says daylight saving begins at 2 am on the first Sunday in October, when clocks move forward one hour; in 2026 that Sunday is 4 October.[D1] Exact market operation, stalls, produce, parking, weather and Sunday sunset time require fresh checks. ## S4-WEB — complete anchor draft

**Proposed path:** `/journal/spring-reset-weekend-3-4-october-2026/` — not live.

**Search title:** Mornington Peninsula weekend plan: 3–4 October 2026 | Peninsula Insider

**Meta description:** Build the last school-holiday weekend around Red Hill market on Saturday morning and the daylight-saving clock change on Sunday.

**Headline:** Fix two points. Leave the weekend room.

**Standfirst:** Put one official market in Saturday morning. Keep Sunday evening clear when the clocks move forward. Let the middle stay loose.

### Saturday morning: the fixed point

Mornington Peninsula Shire lists Hill & Ridge Community Market at Red Hill Recreation Reserve on Saturday 3 October from 9 am to 2 pm.[H1]

Make the market the morning anchor, not a promise about the entire day. The official listing describes makers, growers, food, art, music and vintage goods, but PI has not independently confirmed the stall list, produce, parking or weather plan for this date. Check the event page again before travelling.

Arrive with one rule: buy only what improves the weekend. That might be lunch, something for the table or nothing at all. The market earns its place as a fixed time and location; it does not need a forced itinerary around it.

### Leave the middle loose

Do not pre-book Saturday afternoon simply because the morning is planned. Decide after the market whether the day needs lunch, a short stop or home.

The same applies on Sunday. Keep the middle available for weather, energy and whatever came home from Red Hill. A loose middle is not missing content. It is the design that stops a weekend becoming a transfer schedule.

### Sunday evening: protect the later clock

Business Victoria says daylight saving begins at 2 am on the first Sunday in October, when clocks move forward one hour. In 2026, that is Sunday 4 October.[D1]

The clock change does not create more daylight. It shifts the existing light later on the clock. Protect the evening from an automatic booking and decide close to the day whether it belongs outdoors, at the table or simply at home.

No exact sunset appears in this draft because it has not been checked for a destination and date. Weather and access are also unknown. If a future release recommends a particular lookout, beach or walk, that place needs its own current conditions and access review.

### The two-point plan

1. **Saturday morning:** Hill & Ridge Community Market, subject to a fresh official check.
2. **Sunday evening:** leave the later clock unclaimed until weather and energy are known.

Everything between them is optional.
