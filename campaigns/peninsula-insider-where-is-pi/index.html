THE DECISION

Release Week 1 to scheduling?

Everything below is built and verified. Two holds actually gate a release: legal review of the prize terms, and written confirmation from Doot Doot Doot / Jackalope. Until both clear, nothing can be scheduled.

Default if there is no answer: the pack stays held and nothing is scheduled.

  • These are review renders. Every export carries the REVIEW ONLY badge in its pixels. Release files come from node tools/social-assets/render-where-is-pi.mjs --release, which writes to assets/exports/release/ and refuses to produce a watermarked file.
  • The campaign shows four Peninsula places, none of them on the Case 01 route beyond Mornington, which the game itself gives away on its title card. Sorrento, Flinders and Arthurs Seat are the wrong turns the game already authors, so the campaign misdirects exactly as the game does.
  • The prize sits under the call to action on eight tiles, never as a headline.
