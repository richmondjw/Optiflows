Rowers race toward a finish line they cannot see. That observation sits at the heart of 776BC’s As One / The Pursuit. It is specific to the sport, and strong enough to carry beyond its first telling. Post 3 of 3The methodLinkedIn · single image

The system is in the decisions.

Explain the repeatable editorial decision path without an output or performance claim.

Caption ready for review

The useful part of a content system is the sequence of decisions. Start with a story worth carrying. Draft only the versions that serve a purpose. Check each one for meaning, context and brand. Then decide what is ready to distribute. That is the operating model this 776BC case study is designed to show. The goal is consistency and human control as the story moves across formats, not a bigger asset count for its own sake. See the original Pursuit story: https://optiflows.com.au/776bc/pursuit/ #776BC #EditorialSystems
