# Optiflows homepage rebuild v2

Status: approved for production publication
Date: 2026-09-04

## Strategic decision

The homepage will not lead with "AI-native operating systems" as the category. That phrase still describes part of what Optiflows builds, but it no longer differentiates the firm.

The homepage will lead with the buyer's unresolved problem: **coordination drag**. Optiflows will own the sequence:

1. Measure where work is lost between people, tools and decisions.
2. Redesign the highest-cost workflow.
3. Install the agent system, controls and operating rhythm.
4. Transfer ownership to the client team.

The core proposition is:

> Optiflows measures coordination drag, redesigns the workflow, and installs governed agent systems your team can own.

## Why this position

- McKinsey's 2025 State of AI research found that workflow redesign was the factor most associated with EBIT impact from generative AI. Tool deployment alone is not the value story.
- Deloitte's 2025 Australian SMB research found that only 5% of surveyed AI-using SMBs were fully enabled and that not knowing where to start was the leading barrier among non-users.
- Gartner's 2025 B2B buyer research found that 61% of buyers prefer a rep-free experience, but want seller input for fit and contextual judgement. The page therefore needs to teach, qualify and demonstrate the method before asking for a call.
- Hinge's 2026 professional-services research reports that high-growth firms are 2.5 times more likely to activate visible subject-matter experts. The site must make James and his thinking visible.
- Edelman and LinkedIn's 2025 study found that high-quality thought leadership can make hidden buyers advocate for lesser-known suppliers. Optiflows needs a sharp, evidence-backed point of view, not generic capability claims.
- The internal August 2026 competitive analysis found that "AI operating system", governance controls and fractional AI leadership are already occupied positions. It identified coordination drag, handover as an outcome and governance as an operating method as the defensible territory.

## Buyer

Founder-led and operator-led Australian companies with roughly 10-50 people that have outgrown manual coordination. They already use modern software and have usually experimented with AI. Their problem is not access to tools. It is the absence of a reliable operating design around those tools.

Primary buying situations:

- The founder or operations lead is still the routing layer for routine decisions.
- Work crosses too many inboxes, meetings, spreadsheets and chat threads.
- AI experiments save individual time but do not change the end-to-end workflow.
- Leadership cannot name the cost, risk or ownership model for an AI build.

## Information architecture

1. **Position**: measurable coordination drag, not another AI tool.
2. **Demonstrate**: a scroll-driven workflow model shows fragmentation, measurement, redesign and governed flow.
3. **Self-qualify**: four recognisable buying situations.
4. **Capabilities**: workflow intelligence, agent systems, integration and knowledge, governance and adoption.
5. **Engage**: Coordination Drag Audit, System Install, Operating Partner.
6. **De-risk**: exact artefacts, written success criteria, human authority gates and client ownership.
7. **Trust**: practitioner profile and authored thinking.
8. **Convert**: a specific audit enquiry, not a generic contact form.

## Motion concept

Motion is evidence, not decoration. A single spatial workflow model changes state as the user scrolls:

- fragmented tasks and overloaded handoffs;
- friction points isolated and measured;
- work rerouted into a legible system;
- human authority gates made visible;
- the final system running in a calm, repeatable rhythm.

The model is deterministic and reversible: scrolling up deconstructs the system. Reduced-motion and low-power modes receive a legible static final state. The logo is not animated.

## Evidence and claims policy

- Do not invent client metrics, testimonials or logos.
- Label the workflow visual as illustrative.
- Use externally sourced market data only with a visible source and date.
- Service names, durations and commercial structure were approved for publication on 2026-09-04.
