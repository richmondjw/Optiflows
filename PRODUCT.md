# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are M2M Group marketing, product and sales leads who need to turn a rough campaign idea into a shared production brief. Secondary users are brand approvers, technical reviewers and creative production partners who consume the compiled pack.

The job is to capture the commercial decision, audience problem, proof and delivery constraints once, then produce a consistent handoff for landing pages, social, email, paid media, sales enablement and Higgsfield creative work.

## Product Purpose

The Campaign Brief Compiler is the master input for reusable M2M and Semtech campaign packs. It makes the brief interactive, keeps brand decisions separate from campaign strategy, surfaces missing evidence and gates, and exports a reviewable pack that a team can approve before activation.

Success means a planner can complete a brief without a template expert, see the consequences of each decision immediately, create independent brand branches, and hand production a complete evidence-aware pack. It does not publish, spend, send or call a provider without an explicit human gate.

## Positioning

One campaign brief becomes the source of truth for every production-ready campaign output, with brand rules added as replaceable plugins.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical and technically credible. The interface should feel like a calm control room for a consequential marketing decision: direct language, visible status, useful defaults and no theatre.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic form-builder look, decorative dashboards, hidden validation, unexplained marketing jargon, combined brand lockups, and “AI magic” controls that conceal the source, owner or approval state of an output.

## Design Principles

- Capture the decision before the deliverable.
- Keep the generic campaign core independent from brand identity.
- Show evidence, uncertainty and approval gates beside the claim they govern.
- Make every output inspectable, downloadable and attributable.
- Let one brief create many channel objects without silently changing the strategy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the tool: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, labelled form fields, sufficient contrast, no colour-only status, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Exported campaign content must preserve alt-text, caption and legal-review fields.
