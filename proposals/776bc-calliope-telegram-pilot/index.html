Proposal

I recommend a contained pilot that makes the existing Calliope copywriting capability available to selected 776BC users through Telegram.

This is not a rebuild of Calliope and it is not a general-purpose chatbot. The project packages the existing brand intelligence, rules and workflows into a dedicated agent with a tightly defined catalogue of outputs, clear access controls and mandatory human review.

Recommended decision

Approve a 20-hour development pilot delivered over two weeks, followed by four weeks of controlled use and review.

01

Proposed solution

The pilot places a simple Telegram interface in front of the existing Calliope capability. Each technology retains a distinct role, which keeps the solution understandable, controlled and easy to extend later.

ComponentRole in the pilot
TelegramThe user channel for briefs, source files, questions, draft delivery and revisions.
Hermes Agent HarnessThe isolated runtime that manages Calliope's conversations, skills, permissions, memory and model access.
Calliope agent packageThe identity, operating instructions and approved skills that determine how the agent works.
Approved Obsidian canonA read-only export of current brand rules, product facts, approved examples and channel guidance.
Language-model layerA provider-neutral primary model and fallback selected through testing on real 776BC examples.
n8n and FlowstateExisting deterministic workflows, structured hand-offs and integrations used only where required.
WorkbenchThe existing review surface for outputs that require formal approval or downstream production.
Supabase and GitHubOperational records, evaluation evidence, version control, deployment and rollback.

How it will work

Brief

An authorised user asks for an approved deliverable and supplies the required source material.

Clarify

Calliope checks the brief and asks for missing information before writing.

Create

The relevant skill loads the approved rules and produces a structured draft.

Check

Calliope reviews brand alignment, factual claims, completeness and channel requirements.

Review

The user receives the draft in Telegram or submits it to Workbench for formal review.

Learn safely

Feedback is recorded for evaluation but does not automatically rewrite the brand canon.

Recommended Telegram setup

Begin with direct messages for a small named user group. Add a shared Telegram group only after access controls, conversation isolation and review behaviour have been proven.

Future channel portability

Telegram is the pilot channel, not a permanent platform boundary. The Calliope agent package will remain separate from the channel adapter so it can later be connected to Slack, Microsoft Teams or another supported chat channel without rebuilding the core agent. Each new channel will still require its own access, privacy, identity and approval checks.

02

Proposed launch catalogue

The agent should launch with three to five approved deliverable types. Each selected deliverable will have a fixed brief, output structure, brand checks and escalation rules.

Candidate deliverableDefined output
Campaign messaging packProposition, message hierarchy, campaign angles, headline options, key copy blocks and calls to action.
Landing page or web copyHero copy, supporting sections, proof points, calls to action and SEO title and description.
Email or EDMSubject-line options, preheader, body copy, call to action and an optional follow-up version.
Social copy packChannel-ready variants for agreed platforms, including hooks, captions, calls to action and length controls.
Founder or athlete postA draft in an approved personal voice, grounded only in supplied experience, facts and source material.
Product or collection copyProduct descriptions, benefits, feature language, category introductions and approved supporting facts.
Creative hand-off briefCopy-led concept, visual direction, image-prompt guidance and required text for Pixel or a designer.
Brand review and rewriteAssessment against the canon, identified issues and a corrected on-brand version.

Workloads the agent can perform

  • Turn an approved brief and source material into a first draft.
  • Identify missing information and ask targeted clarification questions.
  • Rewrite existing copy into the 776BC brand voice without changing the underlying facts.
  • Adapt a master message across approved channels, audiences, regions or voices.
  • Create controlled variations for headlines, hooks, subject lines and calls to action.
  • Repurpose a hero asset, transcript or campaign brief into a defined derivative copy set.
  • Review copy against brand rules, prohibited language, channel requirements and approved claims.
  • Package completed copy into a structured hand-off for Workbench, Flowstate or a human reviewer.
  • Revise an output from specific reviewer feedback while preserving the approved strategy and facts.
03

Guardrails and authority

The pilot is intentionally designed as a bounded drafting capability. Calliope may create and revise copy, but it does not own strategy, factual approval or publication.

  • Access is limited to named Telegram users and the deliverable types approved for the pilot.
  • The agent can read only a curated, read-only Calliope canon; financial, legal, shareholder and unrelated confidential material is excluded.
  • The agent must not invent claims, product facts, quotations, customer details or athlete experiences.
  • Missing or conflicting information must trigger clarification or escalation, not a confident guess.
  • The agent cannot publish or release content; every output remains a draft until a nominated 776BC reviewer approves it.
  • User feedback cannot automatically alter the brand canon, skills or operating rules.
  • Model, canon and skill versions are recorded so material outputs can be traced and reproduced.
Included in the development pilot
  • Confirm users, priority deliverables, workloads, approval pathway and success measures.
  • Create an isolated Hermes environment and controlled Telegram access.
  • Translate the Calliope identity and rules, with a curated read-only canon.
  • Build and test three to five deliverable skills and the minimum required hand-offs.
  • Run functional, voice, factual-boundary, access and failure tests.
  • Provide a short operating guide, evaluation process and handover.
Not included
  • Autonomous publishing
  • Community management or daily social execution
  • Visual production or full Pixel orchestration
  • Whole-vault access
  • New Flowstate workflow development
  • Slack, Microsoft Teams or other additional channel connections during this pilot

Delivery sequence

Week 1

Set up and decide

Confirm scope and decisions; configure Telegram and Hermes; establish identity, permissions and the approved canon boundary.

Week 2

Build and release

Build the selected skills; connect required hand-offs; test with real examples; refine and release to pilot users.

Weeks 3 to 6

Use on live work, record output quality and edits, then decide whether to refine, extend or stop.

04

Questions for Kate

The technical setup is straightforward. The important design decisions are what Calliope will be allowed to produce, what work should be prioritised and where human judgement must remain in control.

  1. Launch deliverablesWhich three to five candidate deliverables should be available when the pilot goes live?
  2. Priority workloadsWhich recurring copy tasks consume the most team time or create the greatest bottlenecks today?
  3. VolumeRoughly how many requests of each priority type would the team expect in a normal week?
  4. UsersWho should have access during the pilot, and who should have administrator rights?
  5. VoicesWhich voices are required at launch: 776BC brand, Kate, Cam, athlete, campaign or another defined voice?
  6. BriefingWhat information must a user provide before Calliope is allowed to draft each deliverable?
  7. Source materialWhich existing canon files, approved examples, product facts and campaign materials should form the launch knowledge set?
  8. ReviewWho approves each output type, and should drafts remain in Telegram or move into Workbench?
  9. BoundariesWhat claims, topics, customers, products, markets or language should always be refused or escalated?
  10. SuccessAfter four weeks, what evidence would make the pilot successful: time saved, quality, usage, first-draft acceptance or another measure?
Recommended starting position

Start with three users in direct-message mode and four deliverables: campaign messaging packs, email or EDM copy, landing-page copy and social copy packs. Add founder or athlete voice, product copy and creative hand-off briefs only after the relevant source material and approval rules are complete.
