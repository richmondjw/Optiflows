01 / Food & water

Simple meals. Fresh water.

MEALTIMES

One pouch, twice daily.

Feed Henry in the morning and evening.

Where to find his food

Pouches and dry food are in the lower far-right laundry cupboard. Dry food is also available, but the amount hasn't been confirmed; ask James or Emma before adding it.

THE LITTLE EXTRAS

2-3 treats in the whole day.

Walking treats count toward this daily total.

Treats & water details

Treats are in the far-left kitchen cupboard. Keep fresh water available always; check and refill whenever you go outside and before you leave.

Water is an all-day essential. A checklist tick never replaces checking his bowl.

02 / Out & about

A walk, a sniff, a little adventure.

~30 minutes daily

A second walk is lovely if it's convenient. Henry must always be on lead.

Before you head out
  • Bring a few treats, within his 2-3 daily total.
  • Bring the green poo bags attached to his lead.
  • Pick up after him and use an appropriate waste bin.
HARNESS, GENTLY

Over the head. Around the belly.

Putting it on & taking it off
  1. Slip the harness gently over Henry's head.
  2. Clip it around his belly. Don't force it.
  3. After the walk, unclip the belly strap, then gently slip the harness off.
If he pulls

Henry can pull on walks. Use gentle, reward-based encouragement and keep him on lead.

03 / Home comforts

His favourite thing? Being near you.

A cuddle goes a long way.

Henry is affectionate and likes to stay close to people. Give him a morning cuddle and some company throughout the day. His toys are by the couch.

Settling down.

His usual sleeping place is outside. Sleeping inside is okay if you're comfortable with that.

Bedtime cue & optional biscuit

Try: "Outside, Henry. Come on, Henry."

A small dry biscuit at bedtime is optional.

04 / Before you leave

One last little check.

  1. Henry is in his usual outdoor area.
  2. Check and refill his fresh water.
  3. Make sure the side door is secure.
  4. Be careful closing the front door: hold it until the latch engages, then check it's securely closed.
