Inside an Operating Audit: a worked example

Example workflow. Sales agrees a discount by email. Finance sees the order in the CRM, but can’t see who approved it. Dispatch waits while both teams check.

What the map reveals. The missing handoff is the approval record. Give it an owner and a place on the order that finance and dispatch can both see.

How to test the fix. Count orders held for approval and time from sign-off to dispatch before and after the change. Your audit uses examples from your team.

How we work together

What you can hire me to do

  1. Map the handoffs

    The first paid engagement is an Operating Audit. I trace the work with the people doing it, from the request through to the final decision.

  2. Decide what to fix first

    You get a workflow map, a ranked list of friction points and a recommendation you can take to your team. We agree the scope before you commit.

  3. Put the change into practice

    Your team can use the recommendation itself or ask me to build the first change. We test it on a real handoff and walk the people doing the work through it.
