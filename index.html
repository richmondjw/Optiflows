Most AI guidance assumes you have a data science team, months of runway, and tolerance for failed experiments. Mid-market businesses have none of those. Principles

Five rules. Non-negotiable.

Narrow scope. Real results.

We start with one to three high-friction workflows — not a company-wide transformation. One working system beats ten half-built ones. Scope discipline is the only way pilots become permanent.

Weeks, not quarters

If there's nothing measurable in six weeks, the scope is wrong. Fast proof isn't just about impressing stakeholders — it's what turns a first system into a second, and a second into a practice.

Fit around the business, not the other way around

We wire into the tools your team already uses. No new platforms to learn. No workflows ripped out and rebuilt from scratch. Staff resistance is usually a design failure — we don't create it.

Guardrails from day one

Every system we build has governance designed in from the start — not bolted on when something goes wrong. Audit trails. Human-in-the-loop checkpoints where it matters. Explicit boundaries for what the agent can and cannot do. Mid-market businesses can't absorb governance debt. We don't create it.

One use case. Lasting capability.

The first system isn't just a fix for one problem. It's proof of a pattern. We document everything — the architecture, the decisions, the playbook — so your team can apply it to the next workflow without us. That's the difference between a vendor relationship and an internal capability.
