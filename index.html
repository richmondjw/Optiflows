How I work

Design the work first. Then automate it.

Design thinking, applied to how your business runs.

  1. Watch the work and map it

    I trace one process with the people who do it, from the first request to the final decision.

  2. Redesign the handoff and test it

    We agree who owns each step, then try the change on a real job. If a simpler process fixes it, there’s nothing to buy.

  3. Automate what needs no judgement

    I connect the tools you already use. AI drafts and sorts; a person still approves what matters.

Questions people ask first

What happens to our data?

I work inside the tools and accounts you already own. Before any of your information goes to an AI service, I tell you which service, what information and why, and you decide.

What does AI actually do in the work?

Routine reading and writing: drafting a reply, sorting requests, summarising a long thread. Decisions about money, customers and staff stay with a named person.

What if the answer isn’t technology?

Then that’s the recommendation. Often the fix is agreeing who owns a step, or removing one.
