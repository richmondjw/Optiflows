Step 01

Operating Audit

Find the drag before you pay to fix it.

Most businesses can feel that something is broken. They can't always name it precisely enough to fix it. The Operating Audit is a two-week diagnostic that traces how work actually moves through your business — not how the org chart says it should. We find the two or three workflows costing you the most, quantify what they're costing you in time and margin, and give you a clear brief for what to build first. You leave with a decision, not a deck.

Duration 2 weeks
Price $5,500 + GST · Fixed fee · 100% upfront
Best for Businesses 15–150 people who know something is broken but haven't pinned down exactly what to fix first

The Process
01
Kick-off
Day 1–2

90-minute session with your MD and ops lead. We review your tool stack, hear where work breaks, and identify the 4–5 workflows to trace in depth.

02
Stakeholder interviews
Day 3–4

We interview the people doing the work — not just the people managing it. We trace each workflow from trigger to output and map where value leaks.

03
Analysis
Day 5–8

We score every friction point by frequency, cost, and fixability. For the top three, we sketch the AI-native future state and build the business case.

04
Readout
Day 10

60-minute session. We present the friction map, the ranked intervention briefs, and one clear recommendation. Then we talk about what comes next.

What You Get
Workflow Friction Map

Every traced workflow mapped visually — triggers, steps, handoffs, failure modes, time cost. The real version, not the org chart version. Shared as a Miro board and PDF.

Friction Scorecard

All identified friction points ranked by frequency, weekly cost estimate, and fixability. A single view of where your operational drag is concentrated.

Intervention Briefs (×3)

One-page brief per priority intervention: what breaks, what the AI-native version does, what it connects to, build complexity, and conservative vs. optimistic ROI.

Business Case

Two-page executive summary: annual cost of current friction vs. cost to fix, payback period, what success looks like at 90 days and 12 months.

One Clear Recommendation

Not three options. One recommendation — which workflow to build first, why, and what the System Install brief looks like. You leave with a decision, not homework.

Step 02

System Install

A working operating system in six weeks.

The System Install takes the highest-friction workflow identified in your Audit and rebuilds it as an AI-native operating system — end to end. We design it, build it, wire it into your existing stack, and hand it over to a team that understands how it works and can operate it themselves. Fixed scope. Fixed cost. No pilots. Production.

We do not do open-ended time-and-materials builds. Scope is agreed in writing at the end of Week 2, before build begins. Everything outside that scope is a separate conversation.

Duration 6 weeks
Price From $18,000 + GST · Fixed scope · 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
Best for Businesses with a named internal owner, decision-maker buy-in, and a specific workflow ready to fix

Step 03

Operating Partner

Embedded. Ongoing. Compounding.

The hardest part of AI implementation isn't the first system. It's the second, third, and fourth — and building the organisational capability to keep going. The Operating Partner is where the investment compounds. We stay embedded to install new systems every quarter, tune what's running, and systematically build your team's capability to operate and eventually design new systems themselves.

This is by application. Not every System Install client is the right fit. The conversation happens at the Install handoff. The goal of every Operating Partner engagement is to eventually make OptiFlows unnecessary for the routine work.
