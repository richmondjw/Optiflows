Coordination Drag

The cost isn't in the tools. It's in the gaps between them.

Every growing business hits the same wall. Work stalls at handoffs. Context gets lost between systems. The team spends hours a week chasing status, re-entering data, and rebuilding things that should already exist. This is coordination drag — and it's eating your margin, your speed, and your people.

  • 01

    Broken handoffs

    Work leaves one person or system and arrives somewhere else wrong, late, or incomplete. Every handoff is a potential failure point nobody has time to audit.

  • 02

    Invisible leakage

    Deals not followed up. Clients not renewed. Decisions made without the right information. The value is there — it just never reaches the outcome.

  • 03

    Founder in the loop

    When the system doesn't work, people escalate to whoever makes decisions. That's usually you. And every interruption costs more than it looks.

  • 04

    AI that doesn't stick

    Tools get trialled. Pilots get launched. Nothing changes operationally. The gap between AI excitement and actual workflow improvement stays wide.

How It Works

Diagnose. Install. Embed.

Every engagement follows the same progression. You always know where you are, what comes next, and what you'll have when we're done.

Diagnose
Week 1–2

We interview the people doing the work, trace workflows from start to finish, and find where value leaks. No assumptions. Just what's actually happening — and what it's costing you.

Design
Week 3

We spec the operating system: what it does, what it doesn't do, where humans stay in the loop, how it connects to your existing tools. You approve before we build anything.

Install
Week 4–6

We build and wire it in. Your team trains alongside us. We don't hand off a black box — we hand off a system your team understands and can modify themselves.

The Optiflows standard

Every system we build ships with a governance layer built in from day one — not retrofitted. Audit trail. Human approval checkpoints for high-stakes outputs. Explicit agent boundaries. If something breaks, you'll know what happened, why, and how to fix it.

Non-Negotiables

How we work. No exceptions.

We're practitioners, not consultants. These rules aren't marketing — they're how every engagement runs.

Narrow entry. Measurable results.
