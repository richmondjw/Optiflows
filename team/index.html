How the system holds together

Specialists, not silos.

Every role has a clear lane. The team shares evidence, hands work to the person best placed to carry it forward and keeps approval with the people who own the decision.

  1. 01
    Clear briefs. The work begins with a real outcome, not a vague request.
  2. 02
    Visible hand-offs. Specialist input moves with context, evidence and an accountable next step.
  3. 03
    Human authority. The system supports judgement. It does not replace it.
