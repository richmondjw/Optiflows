How I work

One campaign, from plan to result.

I do the work as well as the plan. You deal with one person throughout.

  1. Agree the priority and plan the campaign

    We settle who it’s for, what we’re offering, which channels to use and what counts as a result.

  2. Build it and run it

    I write and publish the content, set up the audiences and email journeys, and check tracking, search and accessibility before anything goes live.

  3. Measure it and improve the next one

    You get a plain report on enquiries, pipeline and where enquiries stalled. We keep what worked and change what didn’t.

Questions people ask first

Which tools do you work in?

The ones you already have. That usually means your website, an email or marketing automation platform such as HubSpot, LinkedIn and Google Ads. I won’t ask you to buy a new platform to run one campaign.

Do you replace our marketing person or agency?

No. I work alongside whoever you have, and with your sales lead and subject experts. If you have nobody, I cover the campaign from start to finish.

How do you report results?

Against the result we agreed at the start. Enquiries and pipeline come first. Clicks and impressions are only there to explain them.

Can I see prices?

Yes, for ongoing work. The on-demand package shows its weekly price and every option, and you can build yours online. A single campaign is quoted as one fixed fee once we’ve agreed the scope. See on-demand packages.

How is this different from your workflow work?

It’s the same method applied to marketing. If a campaign shows that the real hold-up is a handoff inside the business, I’ll tell you. See how I design workflows.
