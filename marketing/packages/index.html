Questions people ask first

Is advertising spend included?

No. The weekly fee covers the work. You pay LinkedIn and Google directly for the ads themselves, and we agree that budget in the plan.

Who does the work?

I do. I plan it, write it and run it, and I use AI to draft and to speed up production. Nothing is published until I’ve checked it, and you approve anything that goes out under your name for the first time.

Can I change the package later?

Yes. Add or remove extras at any time. The change applies from the next invoice.

Can you guarantee leads?

No. Results depend on your offer, your market and your budget. What I do commit to is the work listed here, on time, and a plain monthly report on what it produced.

What if the hold-up is inside the business?

The monthly report shows where enquiries stall. If the cause is a handoff in your business and not the marketing, I’ll say so, and we can fix that separately. See how I design workflows.

What if I only need one campaign?

Then start with a single campaign for a fixed fee. See campaign marketing.
