V1 Zooty Men's — HOCR Launch Strategy

Stage 1: Strategic Marketing Plan for Leadership Approval

Prepared by: Strategos (Orchestrator) with Athena (Strategy) + Argus (Research) Date: 2026-03-17 Status: DRAFT — Awaiting Cameron / Leadership Approval Task: TASK-0007

1. Executive Summary

The V1 Zooty Men's is 776BC's first product designed explicitly for the North American competitive rowing market, launched around the Head of the Charles Regatta — the single largest and most culturally significant rowing event in the United States.

This is not a product launch. It is a market-entry moment.

The strategy positions the V1 Zooty as the performance garment that treats rowing with the same engineering precision that GT and Formula One bring to motorsport. The athlete is the driver. The Zooty is the machine. 776BC is the team behind both.

Core bet: Own the visual identity of HOCR 2026 through a product that elite rowers want to be seen in — not because it is fashionable, but because it signals that the wearer takes the craft seriously.

2. Product Positioning

Positioning Statement

For competitive male rowers in North America who demand gear engineered with the same precision they bring to their training, the V1 Zooty is the performance garment built by a two-time Olympian's brand that treats rowing like a high-performance discipline — not a recreational sport.

Creative Territory: "Engineered for the Start Line"

The V1 Zooty borrows its design philosophy from GT and Formula One racing:

  • Speed — aerodynamic construction, zero-drag seaming, form-hugging fit
  • Precision — every panel placement informed by the rowing stroke cycle
  • Elite engineering — materials and construction at the level the sport deserves
  • Competitive identity — wearing it is a statement about who you are and how you train

This is not "sportswear inspired by motorsport aesthetics." This is the shared principle: when fractions matter, everything is engineered.

What the V1 Zooty Is Not

  • Not lifestyle. Not fashion-forward. Not for the athlete who trains casually.
  • Not discount-positioned. Not urgency-sold.
  • Not a generic rowing unisuit with a new name.

Naming Note

"V1 Zooty" follows 776BC naming conventions. "V1" signals first generation — with built-in architecture for future iterations. "Zooty" is a rowing-native term (the zootsuit/unisuit), reclaimed and elevated.

3. Target Audience

Primary: The HOCR Competitor

Profile: Male, 22–35, competitive club or collegiate rower in the Northeast US. Rows at a club like Community Rowing Inc., Riverside Boat Club, or a collegiate programme. Trains 5–6 days/week. Has raced HOCR before or is preparing for his first entry. Takes the sport seriously enough to invest in kit that performs.

Psychographic: Metric-driven. Knows his 2k erg score to the tenth of a second. Respects brands with genuine athletic pedigree. Sceptical of brands that market to rowers without understanding the sport. Values function, durability, and credibility over aesthetics.

Current wardrobe: JL Racing, Boathouse Sports, generic club-issued kit. Has heard of 776BC through Australian rowing connections or international coverage. Does not yet own any 776BC product.

Entry point: HOCR event activation, targeted digital campaign, word-of-mouth from 776BC-sponsored athletes.

Secondary: The Adam Archetype (Next-Gen)

The 17-year-old competitive male rower (Adam persona) whose entire identity revolves around his erg score and his dream of rowing at Harvard. His parents invest in his development. He wears 776BC unisuits on-water already. The V1 Zooty extends that relationship into his aspirational race-day wardrobe.

Tertiary: Marcus Crossover

The 30-year-old former collegiate rower now in finance/tech who still rows at a masters level and enters HOCR with his old crew. He has spending power ($120K+), appreciates premium product, and responds to brands with authentic athletic heritage.

4. Campaign Concept

Campaign Name: "Built for the Line"

"The Line" operates on three levels:

  1. The start line — where everything you have prepared for is tested
  2. The racing line — the optimal path through the course (borrowed from motorsport)
  3. The line between preparation and performance — the moment where training becomes racing

Narrative Arc

Phase 1 — Preparation (8 weeks before HOCR) Content theme: the work that happens before the start line. Training footage, erg sessions, early mornings. The V1 Zooty is present but not centred — it is part of the athlete's world.

Phase 2 — Reveal (4 weeks before HOCR) Product launch. The V1 Zooty is formally introduced through a hero content piece (short film or longform article). The GT/F1 engineering parallel is made explicit. Pre-orders open.

Phase 3 — Race Week (HOCR event week) On-the-ground activation at HOCR. Athletes wearing the V1 Zooty. Real-time content from the Charles River. The product is no longer being sold — it is being worn by people who matter.

Phase 4 — Aftermath (2 weeks post-HOCR) Race recaps, athlete stories, performance data. Community content from athletes who wore the V1 Zooty at HOCR. UGC amplification.

Hero Content Piece

A short film (3–5 minutes): "The Line"

Concept: Follow 2–3 competitive rowers through their HOCR preparation. Early mornings on the Charles. Erg sessions in the boathouse. Equipment checks. The V1 Zooty is their race-day armour. The film draws the parallel between F1 pre-race preparation and rowing pre-race preparation — both are high-precision disciplines where margins are measured in fractions.

The film positions the athlete as the driver and 776BC as the engineering team. The brand is the guide, not the hero.

"The Line" — Hero Film Creative Brief

V1 Zooty Men's HOCR Campaign — Flagship Content Piece

Prepared by: Apollo (Media Agent) Task: TASK-0008 Date: 2026-03-17 Campaign: "Built for the Line" Format: Short documentary film, 3–5 minutes

1. Creative Vision

"The Line" is a short documentary following 2–3 competitive rowers through their preparation for the Head of the Charles Regatta. It draws a visual and thematic parallel between the precision engineering of Formula One racing and the precision discipline of competitive rowing.

The film is not a product advertisement. It is a story about what it means to prepare — to engineer yourself, your crew, and your equipment for a moment where fractions of a second matter. The V1 Zooty exists within the film as part of the athletes' world. It is race-day armour, not a product feature.

Core thesis: The line between preparation and performance is where athletes are made. 776BC builds for that line.

2. Narrative Structure

Act 1 — The Work (0:00–1:30)

Theme: Preparation is invisible. The work happens before anyone is watching.

Sequences:

  • Pre-dawn. Alarm at 5:14am. Athlete 1 wakes. Gets dressed in the dark.
  • Drive to the boathouse. Empty roads. Headlights.
  • Boathouse interior. Cold. Erg warm-up. Split monitor glowing. Breath visible.
  • Athlete 2 introduced: weight room. Deadlifts. Controlled power.
  • Athlete 3 introduced: rigging a shell. Checking measurements with a ruler. Precision.
  • On-water: first strokes of the day. Mist on the Charles. The shell glides.

Tone: Quiet. Intimate. The audience is witnessing something private.

Audio: Natural sound only. No music. Erg chain. Oar locks. Water. Breathing. Footsteps.

V1 Zooty presence: Minimal. Seen briefly as athletes change in the boathouse. On-water shots show it naturally.

Act 2 — The Precision (1:30–3:00)

Theme: The engineering parallel. Rowing and motorsport share a principle: when margins are measured in fractions, everything must be precise.

Sequences:

  • Intercut: F1 archival footage (pre-race preparation) with rowing preparation. Same framing. Same angles:
    • Mechanic checking tyre pressure / Rower checking rigger height
    • Driver adjusting mirrors / Cox adjusting microphone
    • Pit crew rehearsal / Crew rowing drills — timing, synchronisation
    • Car on the grid / Shell at the start line
  • Close-ups: V1 Zooty fabric detail, seaming, panel construction — intercut with technical precision shots.
  • Athlete interview fragments (voice only, over footage):
    • "People think rowing is just pulling. It is not. Every degree at the catch matters. Every centimetre of slide. Every fraction of timing between eight people."
    • "The gear has to work with you. You cannot afford anything that fights the movement."

Tone: Building. The pace increases. The parallel becomes undeniable.

Audio: Natural sounds layered with a building low-frequency tone. Still no music. The rhythm of rowing provides the tempo.

V1 Zooty presence: Medium. Product detail shots intercut naturally with the engineering sequences. Not hero-lit. Shot like a component being inspected.

Act 3 — The Line (3:00–4:30)

Theme: The moment. All preparation converges at the start line.

Sequences:

  • HOCR race morning. The Charles River at dawn. Crowds gathering on bridges.
  • Athletes arrive at the venue. Quiet focus. Final preparations.
  • Athlete 1 puts on the V1 Zooty. Pulls it on like armour. Zips up. Looks in the mirror — or does not. Just moves to the dock.
  • Crew carries the shell to the water. Focused. Silent.
  • The start sequence: marshalling, alignment, countdown.
  • The race. Full power. The Charles. Bridges passing overhead. Crowds cheering.
  • Quick cuts: all three athletes in their moments — single, pair, eight. All in V1 Zooty. All racing.
  • The finish. Exhaustion. Hands on knees. A nod between crew members.

Tone: Release. Everything the film built toward is expressed in the race.

Audio: The start builds from silence to full natural sound — coxswain calls, oar strikes, crowd noise, breathing. Then the finish: silence again. Just water.

V1 Zooty presence: Full. This is the payoff. The product is on the athletes in their defining moment. Not featured — worn. The distinction matters.

Closing Card (4:30–5:00)

Visual: Slow fade from water to black. Athlete walking away from the dock, shell on shoulder, V1 Zooty visible. Shot from behind.

Text on screen:

  • "Built for the Line."
  • "V1 Zooty Men's — 776bc.com"
  • 776BC logo

Audio: Water sounds fade to silence.
