The boat moves the wrong way. The rower faces back, watching the water already covered, never the line ahead.

This is the strange geometry of the sport. You commit everything toward a point behind you, a finish you will not see until it arrives. There is no glance forward to check progress, no reassurance mid-race. The work resolves into a decision. Build a crew. Learn to trust them with the line. The athlete who races well is not always the one who pulls hardest. It is the one who commits to a result they cannot watch, and holds that commitment for the full distance. That is the quieter discipline rowing asks for.
