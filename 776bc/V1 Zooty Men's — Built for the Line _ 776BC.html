Section 01 — Engineering

When fractions define the outcome, everything is engineered.

In Formula One, a tenth of a second separates first from forgotten. In rowing, it is no different. The stroke. The catch. The drive. Every movement calibrated, every margin earned.

The V1 Zooty was built with that same discipline. Not designed in a marketing studio. Engineered by a two-time Olympian's brand that understands what athletes need when the margins close and the stakes rise.

Zero-drag seaming. Aerodynamic panel construction informed by the rowing stroke cycle. Compression architecture that supports without restricting. Every element serves the movement.

This is not sportswear inspired by motorsport. This is the shared principle: precision in pursuit of performance.

Section 02 — Specifications

The details that earn their place.

Panel Construction
Mapped to the rowing stroke cycle — each panel placement follows the body's movement path through catch, drive, and recovery.
Zero-Drag Seaming
Flatlock construction eliminates friction at every contact point. No seam fights the stroke.
Compression Fit
Graduated compression supports core and legs without restricting the hip angle at the catch.
Fabric
Lightweight performance fabric with four-way stretch and moisture management. Built to endure hundreds of sessions.
Design Language

Built by athletes.

The V1 Zooty is the product of that conviction. Every seam, every panel, every material choice was informed by decades of competitive experience and the demands of athletes who push their bodies to the limit.
