The outcome

Help customers see how their teamwear will look across a range and approve it sooner. James will work with Sales, Design and Production to reduce the steps from customer brief to approved artwork while preserving the design detail the factory needs.

Where we start

Together we will trace one completed teamwear order from the original brief through revisions to production handover. At the first review, the team will see where time went, which steps caused rework and which improvement is worth testing. A first prototype could show how an approved design translates onto a second garment using editable artwork. Design and Production will judge its accuracy.

How we will work each month

James and a named 776BC lead will choose a priority, agree how to judge success and identify the people who will use the change. The lead will provide a suitable project, available files and timely feedback.

James will prototype and refine the improvement with operators, help them use it on real briefs and document what works. At each monthly review, we will consider the work produced, feedback, time used and the next priority. Where records allow, we will compare time to customer presentation and revision effort against the original job.

A larger build or rollout may use future retainer hours where both parties agree its scope and acceptance checks in writing.

Monthly fee and capacity

A$6,000 ex GST per month

Approximately 17 consulting hours
Based on A$350 per hour ex GST

Working sessions, design, testing, implementation support and handover share that allowance.

If a priority needs more time, we will defer another item, move the work to a later month or agree a separate quote. Extra work, software, hosting and specialist costs require 776BC written approval before any additional charge.

OptiFlows invoices at the start of each service month, payable within seven days. The fee reserves capacity for that month. Unused hours expire at month-end. If OptiFlows cannot provide agreed time, 776BC may choose a carry-forward or refund for the shortfall.

Start, renewal and handover

The retainer begins on a mutually agreed date and renews monthly. Either party may stop the next renewal by email before the next service month starts. The current paid month remains available for agreed work and handover.

Sections 11 to 13 of the signed agreement dated 20 February 2026 apply to ownership, use of learnings and public references, confidentiality and liability.

An authorised 776BC representative may accept this proposal in writing. We will then confirm the start date, day-to-day lead and first customer job.
